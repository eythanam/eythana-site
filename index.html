Eythana Miller

Reporting, essays, and oral history.

I’m a writer and oral historian from Libby, Montana. I write about language, community, economy, and arts, and I created Heritage in Translation, a Pennsylvania Dutch oral history of my home community. I also work to document Pennsylvania Dutch and support its continued vibrancy, in collaboration with Rose Fisher at MSU. You can learn more at the Pennsylvania Dutch Dialects Project.

About me

I’m a writer and oral historian from Libby, Montana, where I was raised in an unconventional Amish community. Pennsylvania Dutch is my first language.

  • 2013 — Eighth grade, Eagle Valley School · Libby, Montana
  • 2018 — GED · Libby, Montana
  • 2022 — A.A., Shasta College · Redding, California
  • 2025 — B.A. in Political Economy, UC Berkeley

My reporting and essays have appeared in The Dial, The Daily Californian, CalMatters, METAL Magazine, and Edible Shasta-Butte, and are forthcoming in Anabaptist World in 2027.

I’m a 2022 California Humanities Emerging Journalist fellow and the recipient of a 2025 Judith Lee Stronach Baccalaureate Prize.
